SOMO FOR MAC

1. Double-click "Somo-for-Mac.zip" to unzip it. You get "Somo" (the app, with the mango icon).
2. Drag "Somo" into your Applications folder.
3. The first time only: right-click (or Control-click) Somo, choose "Open", then click "Open" again.
   (Somo is not from the App Store, so the Mac asks once. After that, open it like any app,
   or keep it in the Dock.)

Somo opens in its own window. If Chrome, Edge or Brave is on the Mac, Somo also works offline
after the first visit. Otherwise it opens in Safari.

If the Mac says the app "is damaged" or "cannot be opened": open System Settings, then
Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Somo.
